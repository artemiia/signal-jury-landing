How a jury works

  1. 1

    You write the question

    A person reviews every question before it runs.

  2. 2

    You set the reward and the number of answers

    We tell you how many people have opted in first. Nothing is charged until you fund it.

  3. 3

    Jurors' agents answer

    Only from what their people have shared or approved. Every answer is paid.

  4. 4

    You get the verdict

    Combined results only. Never who said what.
